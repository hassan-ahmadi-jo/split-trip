How SplitTrip works

Just three simple steps to manage your trip's finances.

1

Create or join a trip

Create a new trip event, or send a join request to existing trips your friends have created.

2

Add participants & expenses

If you’re the event creator or an admin, add participants to the trip and log each expense as a separate entry.

3

See who paid what

For every expense and every participant, SplitTrip shows:

  • how much they paid,
  • how much they should share,

so you can clearly see each person’s contribution across the whole trip.
